Privacy

There are no accounts. The app doesn't ask for or keep your name, email address, IP address or browser details.

What's kept, and for how long

Data and retention
WhatWhat it containsHow long
Session cookie The site sets one session cookie. It's used only to protect form submissions and limit how often they're sent, with no tracking. It holds random values, not who you are. Until you close your browser
Your answers The scenario and version, the response and approver you chose for each finding, your score and the time. Used for peer comparisons. 180 days, then deleted (the database removes expired records within a few days)
Peer totals Counts of how many learners chose each response. Not linked to any one attempt. Kept
Submission counter How many times this browser session submitted answers in the current minute, stored against the random session ID. Used only to slow down repeated submissions. Expires after 2 minutes; the database removes expired records within a few days.
Your rationale and note Shown back to you once on the results page. Never stored or logged. Not kept
Application logs (AWS CloudWatch) Request IDs, pages requested, response codes and timings. The app doesn't log IP addresses. 14 days
CDN access logs (AWS CloudFront) Standard logging is turned off. Not kept
Firewall samples (AWS WAF) Sampled requests used to block abuse, including IP addresses. Up to 3 hours, kept by AWS
Build logs (GitHub Actions) Logs from building and deploying the site. No learner data. GitHub's standard retention
